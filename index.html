01 About

Sundar Pichai is an Indian-American business executive and the chief executive officer of Google and its parent company, Alphabet. He grew up in Chennai, India, and developed an interest in technology and engineering at an early age. He earned a degree in metallurgical engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, a master's degree from Stanford University, and an MBA from the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania.

Pichai joined Google in 2004 and became closely associated with the development and growth of products such as Google Toolbar and Google Chrome. He later led product and engineering teams across Google's major services, including Search, Android, Maps, and Gmail. He became Google's CEO in 2015 and took on the role of Alphabet's CEO in 2019. His leadership has focused on making digital tools more accessible, improving products through research and development, and advancing areas such as artificial intelligence and cloud computing. His career is often cited as an example of how education, curiosity, teamwork, and long-term thinking can shape technology used around the world.
